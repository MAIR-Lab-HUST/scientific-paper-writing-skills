## Use notes

- Copy reported values, units, uncertainty conventions, and citation details exactly; record transformations separately.
- Mark a claim **Supported**, **Partially supported**, **Unsupported**, or **Needs test** in the Decision column.
- Split a compound sentence into multiple rows when its parts rely on different evidence.

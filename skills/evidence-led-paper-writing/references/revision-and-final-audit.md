# Revision and Final Audit

Use this reference to diagnose a manuscript, prioritize revisions, or conduct a final evidence-led pass.

## Diagnose before rewriting

Locate the failure at the smallest level that explains it:

- A claim is not testable, is stronger than its support, or lacks scope.
- A method cannot distinguish the intended claim from an alternative.
- Evidence is missing, incompatible, selectively described, or detached from its conditions.
- An interpretation is being presented as an observation.
- A citation, number, comparison, or uncertainty qualifier has lost its source or role.

Repair the broken link rather than polishing surrounding prose. If the underlying record is insufficient, recommend a narrower statement or a specific new test.

## Revision order

1. Align the problem, central claim, method, evidence, and scope.
2. Add or clarify the most decisive evidence and alternatives.
3. Restore exact numerical, citation, and uncertainty details.
4. Improve section flow and local prose only after the argument is stable.

## Final audit

Check every material claim against these questions:

| Check | Passing condition |
| --- | --- |
| Traceability | Its supporting result or source is identifiable. |
| Status | It is clearly observation, hypothesis, or supported explanation. |
| Fit | The method and evidence actually bear on the claim. |
| Alternatives | Material rival explanations are tested, addressed, or disclosed. |
| Scope | Conditions, limits, and transfer boundaries are stated. |
| Fidelity | Numbers, units, citations, and uncertainty match the record. |
| Comparison | Any ranking or performance statement is protocol-matched and supported. |

## Style safeguards

Prefer precise neutral prose over borrowed authorial mannerisms. Remove rhetorical certainty that exceeds the evidence. Keep caveats near the assertion they qualify, not buried in a distant limitations paragraph.

Return focused findings with a proposed repair and the evidence needed to accept it. Use a full ledger only when many claims must be reconciled; otherwise provide the smallest audit note that makes the next action clear.
